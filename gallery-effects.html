Gallery effects

Ten ways to show the work, all built with GSAP and your own images. Scroll, hover, drag and click each one. Tell me the number you like and I'll build it into the site.

01 Clip reveal02 Velocity marquee03 Hover list 04 Parallax columns05 Expand to full06 3D ring 07 Drag canvas08 Card shuffle09 Slice slideshow 10 Horizontal scroll
01

Clip reveal

Scroll into view

Each picture wipes up out of its frame while the image inside settles from a zoom. Cascades in a stagger.

02

Velocity marquee

Scroll fast

Two endless rows drifting in opposite directions. Scrolling speeds them up and leans the pictures with the motion.

03

Hover list

Move the mouse over the names

A plain typographic index. The picture follows the cursor, swaps per row and tilts with the speed of the pointer.

04

Parallax columns

Scroll slowly

Four columns travelling at different speeds as you scroll, alternating up and down.

05

Expand to full

Click a picture

The thumbnail itself grows out of the grid into a full view, then flies back into its slot.

06

3D ring

Drag sideways

The pictures stand in a turning cylinder. Drag to spin it; it coasts and settles back to a slow turn.

07

Drag canvas

Click and drag in any direction

An endless wall you pan around freely, with inertia. The wall shrinks back slightly while you hold it.

Drag to explore
08

Card shuffle

Plays by itself, or click the deck

A deck of pieces. The top card throws off to the side and tucks in at the back.

09

Slice slideshow

Plays by itself, or click

The next picture arrives in vertical strips, alternating from top and bottom.

10

Horizontal scroll

Keep scrolling down

The page pins and the gallery slides sideways as you scroll; each picture drifts inside its frame.
